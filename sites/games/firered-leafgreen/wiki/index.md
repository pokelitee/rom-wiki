---
title: Pokémon FireRed & LeafGreen
---

This wiki covers the retail Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen releases. Coverage
includes Pokémon base data, learnsets, evolutions, move tutors, wild held
items, moves, items, trainers, abilities, wild encounters, locations, and the
type chart. ROM provenance and PokéDB reference content are kept distinct.

Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen are published as one paired retail reference.
The two games are merged by stable record identity: shared records appear
once, while version-specific records state which game provides them.
