---
title: Pokémon Sacred Gold & Storm Silver
---

This wiki covers the complete v1.05 releases of Pokémon Sacred Gold and Storm
Silver by Drayano. It publishes ROM-derived Pokémon, abilities, moves, items,
wild encounters, trainers, detailed locations, move tutors, NPC trades,
specialty shops, Pickup tables, and type data as one paired reference. ROM
provenance and PokéDB reference content are kept distinct.

Sacred Gold and Storm Silver are published as one paired hack reference. Sacred
Gold is compared against HeartGold and Storm Silver against SoulSilver.
Trainers are linked to the maps and scripts that battle them, with their prize
money. Phone rematches, the opening rival battle, and specialty-shop
inventories remain undecoded rather than receiving retail assumptions.
