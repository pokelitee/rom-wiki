This wiki covers the complete v1.05 releases of Pokémon Sacred Gold and Storm
Silver by Drayano. The initial architecture records exact patched-ROM identities
and their retail comparison bases. ROM provenance and PokéDB reference content
are kept distinct.

The hacks will be published as one paired reference with Sacred Gold compared
against HeartGold and Storm Silver compared against SoulSilver. The site is
currently unpublished and does not yet publish extracted game data. Its verified
Pokémon, ability, and move slices are retained as engine coverage until the
paired comparison workflow and additional datasets are ready for publication.
