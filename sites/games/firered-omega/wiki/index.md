---
title: Pokémon FireRed Omega
---

This wiki covers Pokémon FireRed Omega by Drayano. Coverage includes Pokémon
base data, learnsets, evolutions, move tutors, wild held items, moves, items,
trainers, abilities, wild encounters, locations, and the type chart. ROM
provenance and PokéDB reference content are kept distinct.

FireRed Omega is published as a single-game hack reference. Every supported
dataset is compared with the exact USA retail Pokémon FireRed ROM, and the
resulting differences are collected on the Changes pages.
