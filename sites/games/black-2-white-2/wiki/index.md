This wiki covers the retail Pokémon Black 2 and White 2 releases. It includes
Pokémon, abilities, moves, items, wild encounters, trainers, and locations,
with ROM provenance and PokéDB reference content kept distinct.

Pokémon Black 2 and White 2 are built and published as one wiki. The two games
are merged by stable record identity: shared records appear once, while
version-specific records state which game provides them.
