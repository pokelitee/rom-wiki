---
title: Pokémon Black 2 & White 2
---

This wiki covers the retail Pokémon Black 2 and White 2 releases. It includes
ROM-derived Pokémon, abilities, items, moves, the type chart, wild encounters,
trainers, and locations. ROM provenance and PokéDB reference content are kept
distinct.

Pokémon Black 2 and White 2 are published as one paired retail reference. The
two games are merged by stable record identity: shared records appear once,
while version-specific records state which game provides them.
