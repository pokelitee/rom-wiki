This wiki covers the USA retail releases of Pokémon HeartGold and SoulSilver.
The current data-driven slices publish ROM-derived Pokémon personal data,
abilities, moves, items, wild encounters, trainers, map-header locations, and
the Generation IV type chart. ROM provenance and PokéDB reference content are
kept distinct.

HeartGold and SoulSilver are modeled as one paired retail reference and parsed
independently before records are merged by stable identity. The site remains
unpublished while trainer-to-map links and trainer-class payout multipliers
await exact ROM locators and representative publication review.
