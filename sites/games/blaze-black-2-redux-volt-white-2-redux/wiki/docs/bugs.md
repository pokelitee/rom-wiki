These are known bugs and glitches that have minimal impact on gameplay, but may still be noticeable during a playthrough.

In many cases, these issues require significantly more work to fix than is practical, have an unclear underlying cause, or cannot currently be resolved.

## Challenge Mode Level Bug

- **Location:** Almost everywhere
- **Cause:** Game Freak spaghetti code :/
- **Severity:** Medium

Search for **"Challenge Mode Level Bug - Important Info for Challenge Runs!"** in the Trainer Changes document for a full description of the issue.

## Disappearing Sprites

- **Location:** Any PC
- **Cause:** Unknown
- **Severity:** Low

All Pokémon box sprites have been updated to their superior Gen VI counterparts. However, they may occasionally disappear when viewing the **Deposit Pokémon** or **Withdraw Pokémon** menus in the PC.

- This occurs somewhat randomly and is completely harmless.
- The sprites will reappear after exiting and reopening the PC.

## Minor Fossil Issues

- **Location:** Desert Resort
- **Cause:** Known
- **Severity:** Low

When taking multiple Fossils to the woman in Relic Castle, a minor visual bug may occur when selecting which Fossil you would like to revive. This is purely cosmetic and can safely be ignored.

- The issue occurs because the game expects the woman to be located in the museum, but she has been moved.
- Since the relevant script is shared with other parts of the game, fixing it would likely require more extensive changes than the issue warrants.

## Stone Animations

- **Location:** Dragonspiral Tower
- **Cause:** Known
- **Severity:** Very Low

The animation for the Black Stone and White Stone absorbing energy is version-exclusive. As a result, when encountering **Reshiram in Blaze Black 2 Redux** or **Zekrom in Volt White 2 Redux**, the corresponding animation does not play.

- You still get to see the cool 3D cutscene, though!

## Infestation Text

- **Location:** Any battle involving the move **Infestation**
- **Cause:** Known
- **Severity:** Very Low

When a Pokémon takes residual damage from Infestation, the game may display the following incorrect message:

> The Pokémon was hurt by '----'!

- This issue cannot currently be resolved.
- It is entirely cosmetic and has no adverse effect on gameplay.
