# Redux overview

Pokémon Blaze Black 2 Redux and Volt White 2 Redux are reworked versions of
the original Blaze Black 2 and Volt White 2. The Complete releases include
changes to Pokémon, moves, encounters, trainers, items, and progression in
addition to expanded events and difficulty options.

This site documents facts extracted from the exact supported ROMs. For gameplay
guides, detailed design notes, and creator-maintained documentation, see the
[Blaze Black 2 and Volt White 2 Redux wiki](https://smilingzero.github.io/BlazeBlack2ReduxWiki/).
