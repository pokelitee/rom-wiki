This wiki covers the v1.4.1 Complete releases of Pokémon Blaze Black 2 Redux
and Pokémon Volt White 2 Redux by AphexCubed and Drayano. It publishes
ROM-derived Pokémon, abilities, items, moves, the type chart, wild encounters,
trainers, and locations. ROM provenance and PokéDB reference content are kept
distinct.

The two hacks are published as one paired hack reference: shared records appear
once, while version-specific records are labeled explicitly. Every supported
dataset is compared with its exact retail baseline—Blaze Black 2 Redux against
Pokémon Black 2 and Volt White 2 Redux against Pokémon White 2—and collected
on the Changes pages.
