## Following Platinum

This is a separate patch to be applied to a vanilla Platinum ROM that combines both the v1.3.0 of Renegade Platinum and the [Following Platinum](https://pokehacking.com/fangames/following-platinum/) mod.

Big thanks to Mikelan98 and AdAstra for making the Following Platinum mod, and to chensquared for creating the [combined patch](https://www.reddit.com/r/PokemonROMhacks/comments/s4fbhi/complete_renegade_platinum_and_following_platinum/)!

## Spanish Translation

A patch for a Spanish translation of v1.3.0 of Renegade Platinum is available [here](https://pokehacking.com/fangames/renegade-platinum/).
