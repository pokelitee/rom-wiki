This wiki covers the standard v1.3.0 release of Pokémon Renegade Platinum by
Drayano. It publishes ROM-derived Pokémon, abilities, items, moves, the type
chart, wild encounters, trainers, and locations, including linked shops,
gifts, trades, and connections. ROM provenance and PokéDB reference content
are kept distinct.

Renegade Platinum is published as a single-game hack reference. Every supported
dataset is compared with the exact USA retail Pokémon Platinum ROM, and the
resulting differences are collected on the Changes pages.
