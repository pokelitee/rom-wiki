# Data sources

The primary inputs are the v1.1 Complete releases of Pokémon Blaze Black 2
and Pokémon Volt White 2. Clean USA/Europe Pokémon Black 2 and White 2 ROMs
are parsed separately as their exact comparison baselines.

The paired wiki derives Pokémon, Abilities, Items, Moves, Wild Encounters,
Trainers, and Locations directly from the patched ROMs. Shared records appear
once, while version-specific records retain explicit Blaze Black 2 and Volt
White 2 sections.

PokéDB supplies reference names, descriptions, links, and sprites. It is not
used as a mechanics baseline.
