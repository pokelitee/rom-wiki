This wiki covers the v1.1 Complete releases of Pokémon Blaze Black 2 and
Pokémon Volt White 2 by Drayano. It publishes ROM-derived Pokémon, abilities,
items, moves, the type chart, wild encounters, trainers, and locations. ROM
provenance and PokéDB reference content are kept distinct.

The two hacks are published as one paired hack reference: shared records appear
once, while version-specific records are labeled explicitly. Every supported
dataset is compared with its exact retail baseline—Blaze Black 2 against
Pokémon Black 2 and Volt White 2 against Pokémon White 2—and collected on the
Changes pages.
