---
title: Pokémon Emerald
---

This wiki covers the exact USA/Europe retail release of Pokémon Emerald.
Coverage includes Pokémon base data, learnsets, evolutions, move tutors, wild
held items, moves, items, trainers, abilities, wild encounters, locations,
and the type chart. ROM provenance and PokéDB reference content are kept
distinct.

Pokémon Emerald is published as a single-game retail reference. Battle
Frontier facilities, contests, and Berry mechanics are not covered yet.
