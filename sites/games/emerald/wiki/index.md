---
title: Pokémon Emerald
---

This wiki covers the exact USA/Europe retail release of Pokémon Emerald.
Coverage currently includes Pokémon base data, moves, items, trainers,
abilities, wild encounters, locations, and the type chart. ROM provenance
and PokéDB reference content are kept distinct.

Pokémon Emerald is published as a single-game retail reference. Battle
Frontier and a handful of other Emerald-exclusive systems are not covered
yet.
