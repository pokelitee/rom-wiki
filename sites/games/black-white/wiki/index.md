This wiki covers the retail Pokémon Black and White releases. It includes
ROM-derived Pokémon, abilities, items, moves, the type chart, wild encounters,
trainers, and locations. ROM provenance and PokéDB reference content are kept
distinct.

Pokémon Black and White are published as one paired retail reference. The two
games are merged by stable record identity: shared records appear once, while
version-specific records state which game provides them.
